SYSTEM DESIGN

Architectural Flow & Policy Guardrails

How the system processes raw Twitter customer queries through calibration, retrieval, deterministic policy evaluation, and brand response drafting.

STAGE 1

Text Cleaning & Canonicalization

Redacts Twitter handles, normalizes unicode emojis and spacing, removes shortlinks (`t.co`), and collapses punctuation.

➔
STAGE 2

Calibrated Intent Classifier

Sublinear TF-IDF n-grams combined with domain lexicon density features and Platt-calibrated Logistic Regression.

➔
STAGE 3

Vector Space Retrieval

Retrieves top historical resolution pairs from 3,900+ curated interactions to inform grounding and extract verified links.

➔
STAGE 4

Reasoned Escalation Policy

Enforces 8 deterministic and probabilistic gates (Safety, PII, Billing, Hardware, Logistics, Churn, Uncertainty Gating).

➔
STAGE 5

Brand-Compliant Response Synthesis

Drafts empathetic, step-by-step diagnostic actions with verified Settings paths and encrypted DM redirection links (`apple.co/DM`).

🔥

1. Critical Safety Hazards

Immediate halt to device charging/use for swelling batteries, sparks, burning heat, or lifted OLED screens. Routes to Priority Safety DM.

🔒

2. Confidential Authentication (PII)

Strict prohibition against public credential handling. Apple ID lockouts, 2FA code issues, and passwords route strictly to private DM.

💳

3. Transactional & Billing Disputes

Duplicate credit card charges, unauthorized child in-app purchases, and refund audits require private account inspection in DM.

🛠️

4. Irreversible Physical Defect

Component failures that cannot be fixed by resets (cracked glass, shattered cameras, jammed home buttons) route to Genius Bar repair.

🚨

5. Sentiment & Churn Risk

Detects agitated language, competitor defection threats (switching to Android/Samsung), and demands for human supervisors.

⚖️

6. Model Uncertainty Gating

Any query where classifier confidence falls below 0.38 is automatically gated to human triage queues to prevent misrouting.
